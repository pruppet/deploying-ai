## Overview
The Net and Lantern is a smuggling ring of fishermen operating out of Saltmarsh behind the legitimate front of Lantern Bay Fisheries. The Net and Lantern moves untaxed and stolen goods along the coast of Keoland.

## Goals
The Net and Lantern seeks to control all smuggling on the Saltmarsh coast and to keep the Saltwall Garrison too distracted or too bribed to interfere.

## Structure and Members
The Net and Lantern is led by Ossa Brackwater. Its members are mostly fishing captains who carry contraband alongside their catch, landing it at night by lantern signal. New members are usually drawn in through debt.

## Methods
The Net and Lantern hides cargo in salted fish barrels, lands it through the sea cave below the Lantern Bay smokehouse and keeps two sets of ledgers in the counting house.

## Allies and Rivals
The Net and Lantern buys stolen goods from the Saltgrave Reavers. The Net and Lantern is the Saltwall Garrison's main target, though one garrison sergeant is on its payroll.

## Party Standing
The Net and Lantern considers Nell Tidewick a member in debt and the rest of the party hired help. A successful salvage would raise the party's standing considerably.

## Secrets (DM Only)
The Net and Lantern's arrangement with the Saltgrave Reavers depends on keeping quiet about the prisoners the Reavers take, including Tobin Tidewick.
