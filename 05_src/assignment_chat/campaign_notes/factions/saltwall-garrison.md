## Overview
Saltwall Garrison is the Kingdom of Keoland's military post at Saltmarsh, stationed in a stone fort on the headland above the harbour. Saltwall Garrison is responsible for coastal defence and the collection of crown taxes on trade.

## Goals
Saltwall Garrison seeks to end smuggling in Saltmarsh, defend the coast against the Brine-Born and recover crown property stolen by pirates.

## Structure and Members
Saltwall Garrison is commanded by Captain Hedra Voss, a strict career officer, and numbers around forty soldiers. Sergeants handle day-to-day patrols of the docks.

## Methods
Saltwall Garrison searches boats at random, pays informants among the fishing crews and posts lookouts on the headland at night.

## Allies and Rivals
Saltwall Garrison's main opponent is the Net and Lantern. Saltwall Garrison regards the Brine-Born as a growing military threat.

## Party Standing
Saltwall Garrison suspects Nell Tidewick of smuggling and is watching the Soul of Winter through its informant, Fennick Ploud.

## Secrets (DM Only)
Saltwall Garrison has its own records on the Tyrant of the Tides, whose pirates once seized a crown tax shipment. Captain Hedra Voss would claim the wreck's strongbox as crown property if she learned of the salvage. One of her sergeants is paid by Ossa Brackwater.

## Combat
Saltwall Garrison soldiers use the Guard stat block, and Captain Hedra Voss uses the Veteran stat block.
