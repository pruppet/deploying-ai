## Overview
The Brine-Born is a coalition of sea creatures, led by sahuagin, that claims the waters around the Brinejaw Shoals. The Brine-Born has grown bolder in recent months, raiding nets and boats near Saltmarsh.

## Goals
The Brine-Born seeks to drive surface ships away from the reef and to recover sacred objects lost to the sea.

## Structure and Members
The Brine-Born is led by a sahuagin priestess known as the Tide Matron. Its members include sahuagin warriors, giant crabs and other reef creatures that serve them.

## Methods
The Brine-Born raids at dusk and dawn, cuts anchor lines and uses the wreck of the Tyrant of the Tides as a watch post.

## Allies and Rivals
The Brine-Born tolerates Krell Grohlg in exchange for offerings. The Brine-Born treats every surface faction, including the Saltwall Garrison and the Net and Lantern, as an intruder.

## Party Standing
The Brine-Born has no relationship with the party yet, but will treat anyone boarding the wreck as a threat.

## Secrets (DM Only)
The Brine-Born reveres the Moonwake Conch as a holy relic and will not let it leave the reef willingly. The Tide Matron senses something familiar in Mernaid, which could make negotiation possible.

## Combat
The Brine-Born uses the sahuagin stat blocks from Ghosts of Saltmarsh and the Giant Crab stat block.
