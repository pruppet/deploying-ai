## Overview
Fennick Ploud is the youngest member of the Soul of Winter's crew, working as deckhand and cook. Fennick Ploud is a human teenager from Saltmarsh who joined the crew last spring.

## Appearance and Manner
Fennick Ploud is thin and freckled, with a fishing cap two sizes too large. Fennick Ploud asks constant questions, exaggerates every story he tells and is fascinated by Mernaid.

## Motivations
Fennick Ploud wants to see something worth telling stories about. Fennick Ploud fears being sent back to his family's net-mending stall.

## Relationships
Fennick Ploud looks up to Bram Hollins and tries to copy his manner. Fennick Ploud follows Mernaid around asking about life under the sea. Teodora Marsh treats Fennick Ploud as a nuisance she would defend to the death.

## Party Interactions
Fennick Ploud will beg to accompany the party onto the wreck and may stow away aboard the rowboat if refused.

## Secrets (DM Only)
Fennick Ploud is paid a few coins each week by the Saltwall Garrison to report what cargo the Soul of Winter carries. Fennick Ploud does not fully understand what the garrison does with the information.

## Combat
Fennick Ploud uses the Commoner stat block and will flee rather than fight.
