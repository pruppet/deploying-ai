## Overview
Bram Hollins is the first mate of the Soul of Winter, the fishing ship owned by Captain Knowmere. Bram Hollins runs the deck when Knowmere is ashore working as a tracker.

## Appearance and Manner
Bram Hollins is a broad human man in his forties with a shaved head and a sun-faded naval tattoo on one forearm. Bram Hollins speaks in short orders, has little patience for passengers and trusts only people who have hauled nets beside him.

## Motivations
Bram Hollins wants enough coin to buy a ship of his own and stop answering to anyone. Bram Hollins fears being recognised by anyone from the Keoland navy.

## Relationships
Bram Hollins is loyal to Knowmere but considers him too soft with the crew. Bram Hollins bickers constantly with Teodora Marsh over how the ship should be run, and treats Fennick Ploud like a younger brother.

## Party Interactions
Bram Hollins will sail the party to the wreck and back but refuses to board the Tyrant of the Tides, claiming it is bad luck.

## Secrets (DM Only)
Bram Hollins first spotted the Tyrant of the Tides when the Soul of Winter sheltered from a storm near the Brinejaw Shoals, and sold its location to Ossa Brackwater without telling Knowmere. Bram Hollins is also a deserter from the Keoland navy, which the Saltwall Garrison would arrest him for.

## Combat
Bram Hollins uses the Guard stat block, fighting with a boarding axe.
