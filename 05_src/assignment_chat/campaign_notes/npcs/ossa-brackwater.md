## Overview
Ossa Brackwater is the owner of Lantern Bay Fisheries and the leader of the Net and Lantern, Saltmarsh's smuggling ring. Ossa Brackwater commissioned the party to salvage the wreck of the Tyrant of the Tides.

## Appearance and Manner
Ossa Brackwater is a human woman in her sixties with salt-cracked hands, a grey wool shawl and a clay pipe she rarely lights. Ossa Brackwater speaks softly, never raises her voice, and calls everyone "love" until they disappoint her.

## Motivations
Ossa Brackwater wants the iron strongbox from the Tyrant of the Tides, which she believes holds the pirate captain's charts of hidden coves and buried caches. With those charts, the Net and Lantern could move cargo beyond the reach of the Saltwall Garrison. Ossa Brackwater fears a full garrison crackdown more than any rival crew.

## Relationships
Ossa Brackwater holds Nell Tidewick's smuggling debts and has offered to reduce them in exchange for a successful salvage. Ossa Brackwater respects Knowmere's skill as a tracker but quietly suspects he is hiding from something. Ossa Brackwater pays a garrison sergeant to look the other way on certain nights.

## Party Interactions
Ossa Brackwater hired the party at the Lantern Bay counting house, offering a fee, a share of the salvage and a reduction of Nell Tidewick's debt. Ossa Brackwater chartered Knowmere's ship, the Soul of Winter, to carry the party to the wreck.

## Secrets (DM Only)
Ossa Brackwater knows that Tobin Tidewick, Nell's husband, was taken by the Saltgrave Reavers, a pirate crew that supplies the Net and Lantern with stolen goods. Ossa Brackwater has kept this from Nell to protect the arrangement.

## Combat
Ossa Brackwater uses the Spy stat block and avoids fighting personally, relying on hired muscle from the Net and Lantern.
