## Overview
Krell Grohlg is a half-orc and the last surviving pirate of the Tyrant of the Tides. Krell Grohlg has lived alone aboard the wreck for fifteen years, surviving on crabs and other crustaceans gathered from the reef, and has gone partly mad.

## Appearance and Manner
Krell Grohlg is gaunt and barnacle-scarred, wearing the rotted remains of a captain's coat decorated with crab shells. Krell Grohlg talks to the crabs as if they were his crew, gives them names and ranks, and shifts without warning between friendly rambling and sudden violence.

## Motivations
Krell Grohlg believes the Tyrant of the Tides is still his ship and that its crew will one day return. Krell Grohlg wants to defend the ship and its treasure from anyone he sees as a mutineer or thief.

## Relationships
Krell Grohlg has an uneasy truce with the Brine-Born, who allow him to live on the reef in exchange for offerings. Krell Grohlg trades salvaged goods with the Saltgrave Reavers when they anchor at the shoals.

## Party Interactions
Krell Grohlg may first greet the party as new recruits, offering them crab and stale rum, before turning hostile if they open the hold or enter his office.

## Secrets (DM Only)
Krell Grohlg keeps a letter from the captain of the Saltgrave Reavers in his office, which mentions a halfling prisoner named Tidewick. Krell Grohlg also knows where the Moonwake Conch is hidden aboard the ship.

## Combat
Krell Grohlg uses the Krell Grohlg stat block from Ghosts of Saltmarsh, reflavoured as a pirate whose spells draw on the tides and the reef's crustaceans. Krell Grohlg fights alongside giant crabs that obey him.
