## Overview
Teodora Marsh is the bosun of the Soul of Winter, responsible for its rigging, hull and stores. Teodora Marsh is a dwarf who has served on the ship longer than anyone except Knowmere.

## Appearance and Manner
Teodora Marsh is a stocky dwarf woman with a braided copper beard tied with fishing line and a belt of tools she never removes. Teodora Marsh is blunt, practical and openly superstitious about whistling, red sails and singing on deck.

## Motivations
Teodora Marsh wants the Soul of Winter kept seaworthy and her crew alive. Teodora Marsh fears that the wreck's bad luck will follow anyone who takes anything from it.

## Relationships
Teodora Marsh respects Knowmere as a captain and argues with Bram Hollins out of habit rather than malice. Teodora Marsh has asked Mernaid, more than once, to stop singing near the bow. Teodora Marsh shares superstitions and ward-charms with Nell Tidewick.

## Party Interactions
Teodora Marsh can repair gear, rig hoists for heavy salvage and lend the party rope, hooks and a block and tackle.

## Secrets (DM Only)
Teodora Marsh recognised the Tyrant of the Tides from her youth, when its pirates burned the ship she first sailed on. Teodora Marsh has not told anyone and may try to sink the wreck rather than let it be salvaged.

## Combat
Teodora Marsh uses the Guard stat block, fighting with a heavy maul.
