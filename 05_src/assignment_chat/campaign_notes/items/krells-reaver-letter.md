## Overview
Krell's Reaver Letter is a water-stained note in Krell Grohlg's office, written to him by the captain of the Saltgrave Reavers. Krell's Reaver Letter reveals that Nell Tidewick's husband, Tobin Tidewick, was captured alive by a different pirate crew, the Saltgrave Reavers.

## Description
Krell's Reaver Letter is a single folded sheet sealed with red wax pressed with a gull's skull. It is kept in a tin box under Krell Grohlg's desk with other trade papers.

## Text of the Letter
Krell's Reaver Letter reads: "Krell, the usual terms. Rum and powder for whatever the reef gives up. We'll anchor at the shoals at the dark of the moon. Keep the crabs off my boats this time. We have a halfling aboard, Tidewick by name, took him off a Saltmarsh boat. Knows the coast and the Lantern routes better than he lets on. We're keeping him, our friend at the Saltwall Garrison has agreed to cover our tracks. Tell no one. Captain Mags Corrow, Saltgrave Reavers."

## Party Knowledge
Krell's Reaver Letter has not yet been found by the party.

## Secrets (DM Only)
Krell's Reaver Letter mentions the "Lantern routes", which is the first clue that the Net and Lantern has dealings with the Reavers and that Ossa Brackwater may know more about Tobin Tidewick than she has said.
