## Overview
The Moonwake Conch is the family relic of Mernaid, lost when she was swept away three hundred years ago. Recovering the Moonwake Conch is Mernaid's main motivation.

## Description
The Moonwake Conch is a spiral shell the length of a forearm, pale silver with a faint blue shimmer that brightens under moonlight. Sea elf script is carved around its opening.

## Properties
The Moonwake Conch requires attunement by a creature with sea elf heritage. Once per day, the bearer can sound the conch to calm hostile sea creatures nearby or to restore a small amount of health to allies. The exact mechanics are left for the DM to finalise.

## History
The Moonwake Conch was passed down through Mernaid's family for generations. The Moonwake Conch reached the Tyrant of the Tides when its pirates took it from a coastal shrine, and Krell Grohlg has hidden it somewhere aboard.

## Party Knowledge
The Moonwake Conch is known to Mernaid only through family stories. The party does not yet know it is aboard the wreck.

## Secrets (DM Only)
The Moonwake Conch is revered by the Brine-Born, who consider it a holy object. Taking it from the reef will draw their pursuit unless Mernaid can negotiate with the Tide Matron.
