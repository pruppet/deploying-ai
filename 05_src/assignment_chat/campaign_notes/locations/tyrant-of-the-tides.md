## Overview
The Tyrant of the Tides is a pirate ship that vanished fifteen years ago and now lies wedged on the reef of the Brinejaw Shoals. The Tyrant of the Tides is the target of the party's salvage operation.

## Notable Features
The Tyrant of the Tides lists heavily to one side, its masts broken and its hull crusted with barnacles. The deck is slick with weed and littered with crab shells. The lower hold is partly flooded at high tide, and the captain's cabin at the stern has become Krell Grohlg's office.

## Inhabitants
The Tyrant of the Tides is inhabited by Krell Grohlg, the last surviving pirate, and by large numbers of crabs and other crustaceans that he treats as his crew. Brine-Born scouts visit the wreck regularly.

## Services and Resources
The Tyrant of the Tides holds the pirates' iron strongbox, the Moonwake Conch and Krell Grohlg's papers, including a letter concerning Tobin Tidewick.

## Hooks
The Tyrant of the Tides offers three prizes: the strongbox Ossa Brackwater wants, Mernaid's family relic and news of Nell Tidewick's missing husband.

## Dangers
The Tyrant of the Tides is defended by Krell Grohlg and giant crabs, and the Brine-Born consider the reef their territory. The hull shifts with the tide, and disturbing the cargo may cause the wreck to slide off the reef into deep water.

## Secrets (DM Only)
The Tyrant of the Tides ran aground after its crew mutinied during a storm. Krell Grohlg survived by hiding in the hold while the Brine-Born took the rest of the crew.
