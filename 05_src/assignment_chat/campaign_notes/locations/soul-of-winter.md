## Overview
The Soul of Winter is a fishing ship owned and captained by Knowmere. The Soul of Winter carries the party from Saltmarsh to the wreck of the Tyrant of the Tides.

## Notable Features
The Soul of Winter is a sturdy, dwarven-built single-masted vessel with a deep hold for the catch, a small forecastle for the crew and a captain's cabin at the stern. The Soul of Winter carries a rowboat on deck and a winch strong enough to lift heavy nets or salvage.

## Inhabitants
The Soul of Winter is crewed by Bram Hollins (first mate), Teodora Marsh (bosun) and Fennick Ploud (deckhand and cook), alongside Captain Knowmere.

## Services and Resources
The Soul of Winter provides transport, basic supplies, the rowboat for reaching the wreck, and Teodora Marsh's rigging for hauling up heavy cargo.

## Hooks
The Soul of Winter's crew are uneasy about the voyage, and each has a different reason for it.

## Dangers
The Soul of Winter is vulnerable when anchored near the Brinejaw Shoals, where Brine-Born scouts watch from the water.

## Secrets (DM Only)
The Soul of Winter is the last ship of the fleet once owned by Knowmere's father, and Knowmere took it when he fled his home port. Anyone searching for Knowmere could recognise the ship.
