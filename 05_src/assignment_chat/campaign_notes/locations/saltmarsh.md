## Overview
Saltmarsh is a small fishing town on the coast of the Kingdom of Keoland. Saltmarsh is the party's home port and the starting point for the salvage of the Tyrant of the Tides.

## Notable Features
Saltmarsh is built around a curved harbour lined with fishing piers, with marshland stretching inland behind the town. The Lantern Bay Fisheries wharf, with its fish house, smokehouse, net loft and counting house, dominates the eastern docks. The stone walls of the Saltwall Garrison stand on the headland above the harbour mouth.

## Inhabitants
Saltmarsh is home to fishing families, dockworkers, a handful of merchants and the soldiers of the Saltwall Garrison. Ossa Brackwater, Nell Tidewick and her family, and Captain Knowmere all live in Saltmarsh.

## Services and Resources
Saltmarsh offers chandlers, a shipwright, several taverns along the waterfront, a small temple and a market where most of the catch is sold each morning.

## Hooks
Saltmarsh has seen more night landings than usual, and the garrison has begun searching fishing boats at random. Fishermen report nets torn and catches stolen near the outer reefs.

## Dangers
Saltmarsh's outer waters are increasingly claimed by the Brine-Born, and smugglers settle disputes quietly in the alleys behind the wharf.

## Secrets (DM Only)
Saltmarsh's Lantern Bay smokehouse has a cellar with a tunnel leading to a sea cave below the cliffs, used by the Net and Lantern to land contraband.
