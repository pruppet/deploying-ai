## Overview
The Brinejaw Shoals is a reef of jagged rock and coral roughly a day's sail from Saltmarsh, where the wreck of the Tyrant of the Tides lies.

## Details
The Brinejaw Shoals rises close to the surface at low tide and disappears beneath it at high tide, making it extremely dangerous to approach by ship. Ships must anchor outside the reef and send rowboats through narrow channels. Strong currents change direction with the tide, and fog is common in the early morning.

## Common Knowledge
The Brinejaw Shoals is avoided by most Saltmarsh fishermen, who say the reef has teeth. Boats that fish too close often find their nets cut.

## Party Knowledge
The Brinejaw Shoals is known to Knowmere and his crew as dangerous water. The party will need to time their approach to the tide.

## Secrets (DM Only)
The Brinejaw Shoals is the territory of the Brine-Born, who keep a shrine in an underwater cave beneath the reef. The Saltgrave Reavers anchor at the shoals at the dark of the moon to trade with Krell Grohlg.
