## Overview
The Loss of the Tyrant of the Tides refers to the disappearance, fifteen years ago, of the most feared pirate ship on the Saltmarsh coast.

## Details
The Tyrant of the Tides was captained by Varos Thorne, who raided merchant and crown shipping along the coast of Keoland for nearly a decade. The Tyrant of the Tides vanished during a winter storm, and most sailors assumed it had sunk with all hands. Rumours of its treasure have drawn fortune-seekers to the coast ever since.

## Common Knowledge
The Loss of the Tyrant of the Tides is told in Saltmarsh taverns as a ghost story. Fishermen claim to see its lanterns on stormy nights near the Brinejaw Shoals, and many refuse to fish there.

## Party Knowledge
The Loss of the Tyrant of the Tides became relevant to the party when Ossa Brackwater revealed that the wreck had been found intact on the reef.

## Secrets (DM Only)
The Loss of the Tyrant of the Tides was caused by a mutiny during the storm, which drove the ship onto the reef. The Brine-Born took most of the crew, and Krell Grohlg is the only survivor.
