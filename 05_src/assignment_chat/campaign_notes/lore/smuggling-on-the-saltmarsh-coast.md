## Overview
Smuggling on the Saltmarsh Coast is the long-standing practice of moving untaxed and stolen goods through Saltmarsh's fishing fleet.

## Details
Smuggling on the Saltmarsh Coast grew as the Kingdom of Keoland raised taxes on coastal trade. Fishing boats are ideal for smuggling, since they sail at odd hours, carry barrels and are rarely searched thoroughly. Common contraband includes wine, spirits, silk, weapons and goods stolen by pirates.

## Common Knowledge
Smuggling on the Saltmarsh Coast is an open secret in town. Many families rely on it, and most residents see the crown's taxes as the real theft. Few people say the name of the Net and Lantern aloud.

## Party Knowledge
Smuggling on the Saltmarsh Coast is familiar to Nell Tidewick, whose debts come from her husband's smuggling business, and to Knowmere, who has seen night landings from his own boat.

## Secrets (DM Only)
Smuggling on the Saltmarsh Coast is more dangerous than locals believe, because the Net and Lantern's supply of pirate goods depends on the Saltgrave Reavers, who take prisoners from Saltmarsh boats.
