## Overview
Knowmere is a half-elf ranger and fisherman who has lived in Saltmarsh for five years. Knowmere captains the Soul of Winter and hires himself out as a tracker.

## Background
Knowmere grew up in a family of means; his father owned a fishing fleet. Knowmere left his home port suddenly after something happened in his past, and arrived in Saltmarsh with a single ship and no explanation.

## Motivation
Knowmere wants to keep his past behind him while building a life in Saltmarsh.

## Relationships
Knowmere pulled Mernaid out of the sea in his fishing nets. Knowmere and Nell Tidewick are competitors and frenemies, rivals for the same fishing grounds who still look out for each other.

## Abilities and Quirks
Knowmere is skilled at tracking on land and reading tides and currents at sea, and is the party's best navigator.

## Secrets (DM Only)
Knowmere's past remains for the player to reveal. The Soul of Winter is recognisable as a ship from his father's fleet, which could lead pursuers to him.
