## Overview
Nell Tidewick is a halfling rogue born and raised in Saltmarsh. Nell Tidewick took over her husband's fishing ship, the Lucky Wren, after he disappeared, and was drawn into his smuggling business.

## Background
Nell Tidewick cares for her own elderly parents and her husband's parents. Nell Tidewick's parents are supportive, while her in-laws are judgemental about how she runs the ship. The smuggling business left Nell Tidewick with debts to the Net and Lantern, which she is trying to pay off by taking jobs.

## Motivation
Nell Tidewick wants to find out what happened to her husband, Tobin Tidewick.

## Relationships
Nell Tidewick and Knowmere are competitors and frenemies. Nell Tidewick owes money to Ossa Brackwater, leader of the Net and Lantern.

## Abilities and Quirks
Nell Tidewick is highly superstitious, carrying ward-charms, refusing to sail on certain days and reading omens in the weather and the catch.

## Secrets (DM Only)
Nell Tidewick's husband, Tobin Tidewick, has been taken by the Saltgrave Reavers, as revealed in Krell's Reaver Letter aboard the Tyrant of the Tides.
