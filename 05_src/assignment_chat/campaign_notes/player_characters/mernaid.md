## Overview
Mernaid is a half sea elf sorcerer with a divine soul who was lost at sea for three hundred years. Mernaid is trying to learn whether she can live in the surface world.

## Background
Mernaid was swept away from her family three hundred years ago and only recently returned to the surface, where Knowmere caught her in his nets. Much of the world Mernaid remembers no longer exists.

## Motivation
Mernaid wants to recover the Moonwake Conch, her family's relic.

## Relationships
Mernaid owes her return to the surface to Knowmere. Fennick Ploud follows Mernaid with endless questions, and Teodora Marsh has asked her to stop singing on deck.

## Abilities and Quirks
Mernaid can heal fish and other sea creatures. Mernaid has an awful singing voice and sings anyway.

## Secrets (DM Only)
Mernaid's family relic is aboard the Tyrant of the Tides, and the Brine-Born's leader, the Tide Matron, may recognise Mernaid's lineage.
